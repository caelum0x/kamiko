## Reddit (r/vtubers)

**Title:** Built a tool after getting tired of AI VTuber and stock-meme
crypto fans wanting a live interactive character tied to a real market
signal, not a static PFP

For a while I kept running into this: AI VTuber and stock-meme crypto fans
want a live interactive character tied to a real market signal, not a
static PFP.

I'm part of AI VTuber fans, NVDA/stock-meta crypto traders, and $KAMIKO
holders, so I ended up building Kamiko to fix it for myself first. Runs
entirely in-browser with no wallet or signup, talks back by chat or voice,
and her mood reacts live to chat and NVDA signals, plus a tap-to-wake game
and holder perks.

It's up at https://kamiko.fun if you want to poke at it -- genuinely
interested in whether this is a problem other people here have too.

(Note: r/vtubers and most VTuber-adjacent subreddits moderate crypto/token
content closely -- read each subreddit's rules before posting; a
VTuber-tech-focused subreddit may take this better than a general one.)
